# Tarika (تَرِكة): turning Egypt's frozen inherited property into liquid assets

> **One line:** Tarika buys, mediates and manages *undivided inherited property shares* in Egypt. It gives heirs who want out cash in weeks, not years in court, and it puts the millions of empty family homes back on the market.

---

## 1. The problem

Egypt has one of the largest stocks of **dead real estate** in the world:

- **12.8 million vacant housing units** in the 2017 CAPMAS census: about 4.6M finished, 4.3M unfinished and 2.8M closed. In Cairo, **22.7%** of units were empty.
- At the same time, millions of young Egyptians cannot afford a home.

One big, under-discussed cause is **inheritance (الميراث)**:

1. **Islamic inheritance splits property into many small undivided shares (مشاع).** One apartment can end up with 6 to 12 heirs: siblings, a widow, a mother, cousins, and heirs who live in the Gulf or Europe.
2. **Nobody can act alone.** To sell, rent or renovate, all heirs must agree. One heir who refuses, or one who can't be reached, freezes the whole asset.
3. **The only exit is court.** A judicial partition suit (دعوى فرز وتجنيب / قسمة قضائية) can take **years** and usually ends in a forced public auction at a poor price.
4. **Minority heirs have no exit.** A sister with a 1/8 share in a Shubra apartment can't sell it on the open market, because nobody buys a fraction of an apartment co-owned with strangers. Women are hit hardest: withholding women's shares is common enough that Egypt made it a crime (Law 219 of 2017).
5. **Families break apart.** Inheritance fights are among the most common causes of family estrangement in Egypt.

**Result:** Trillions of EGP of real estate is stuck. It earns no rent, gets no maintenance and has no liquidity. Meanwhile, heirs who need cash for education, a marriage, a business or medical bills can't touch their own wealth.

### Why now: a once-in-50-years trigger

In **August 2025**, Egypt ratified the amendments ending the **old rent (الإيجار القديم)** regime:

- **7 years** for residential leases to end, and **5 years** for non-residential leases.
- Steep staged rent increases during the transition.
- After that, units go back to their owners under the Civil Code.

Most old-rent buildings are 50+ years old, and their original landlords have died. **Today those buildings are owned by many heirs.** Over the next 7 years, hundreds of thousands of units will return to ownership groups that can't agree on what to do with them. Tarika is built to be the coordinator, buyer and operator for exactly this wave.

## 2. The solution

Tarika is a **legal-tech + proptech + investment** company with four layers. Each layer feeds the next.

| Layer | What it does | Who pays |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Tarika App (free)** | Sharia-correct inheritance calculator (حاسبة المواريث), a digital "estate file" listing assets, heirs, documents and missing papers, and an instant **AI valuation** of the property *and of each heir's share* | Free. This is the lead engine |
| **2. Estate Concierge** | End-to-end paperwork: declaration of heirs (إعلام الوراثة), real-estate registration (الشهر العقاري), utility and tax transfers, and **power-of-attorney handling for heirs abroad** | Fixed fee, EGP 10k–40k per estate, higher in USD for diaspora |
| **3. Family Buyout & Mediation** | A neutral, data-driven mediator. It gives an independent valuation and helps one heir buy out the others, or all heirs sell together at full market price instead of at auction. Includes Sharia-compliant **installment financing** for the heir who buys | 2–3% success fee + financing margin |
| **4. Tarika Liquidity Fund** | When no family member buys, **Tarika buys the minority share itself** at a transparent, capped discount. It pays cash within ~30 days, then consolidates and **activates** the property | Spread between purchase price and exit value + rental income |

And on top of these:

- **Activation services:** finishing, renovation and long-term rental management for unlocked units (10–12% of rent + finishing margin).
- **Old-Rent Building Program:** heirs of old-rent buildings appoint Tarika as the **single manager**. Tarika handles tenant transitions, collects the staged rents, renovates and re-leases or sells. Heirs who want out sell their shares to the fund.

### How the buyout works (example)

> A Giza apartment is worth **EGP 4.0M**. Five heirs own it. Mona (1/8 share = **EGP 500k** of value) lives in Dubai and wants out. Her brothers refuse to sell and can't afford to buy her out.

1. Mona gets an independent valuation in the app.
2. Tarika **first formally offers her share to her co-heirs**, as Egyptian law gives co-owners pre-emption rights (الشفعة / حق الاسترداد). If they buy, Mona gets cash and Tarika earns a mediation fee.
3. If they decline, **the Tarika Fund buys Mona's share for ~EGP 375k** (a 25% discount, the price of instant liquidity). She is paid in under 30 days, fully remotely.
4. Tarika is now a co-owner, and it acts as a patient, professional one. It offers the other heirs:
   - to buy them out too, then renovate and sell the whole unit at full price; or
   - to rent the unit out and share income pro rata (they receive income they never had); or
   - to sell its share back to them in installments.
5. If no agreement is reached, Tarika (as a co-owner) can bring a partition claim as a last resort. The *credible option* alone usually brings the family to the table.

Everyone wins: Mona gets her money, the brothers get a rational partner instead of a feud, and an empty apartment goes back on the market.

## 3. Why this is unique (competition)

As far as our research shows, **no company in Egypt offers heir-share liquidity or acts as a professional co-owner of inherited property.**

| Player | What they do | Why they're not Tarika |
|---|---|---|
| Nawy, Aqarmap, Property Finder, Dubizzle | Brokerage and listings of *whole* units (Nawy also offers fractional investment in new developments) | Can't list or sell a disputed 1/8 share of a family apartment |
| Law firms / independent lawyers | Litigate partition suits | Slow, adversarial, fragmented and offline. They *profit from* the conflict |
| Traditional brokers (سماسرة) | Informal deals | No capital, no legal structure, low trust |
| Banks / mortgage finance | Lend against clean, registered, wholly owned property | Won't touch undivided, unregistered shares |

Comparable models abroad prove the economics (inheritance-advance and heir-buyout firms in the US, and fractional co-ownership players). No one has built the **Sharia-compliant, Egypt-specific, legally structured** version, and the Egyptian problem is far bigger because of fragmentation, informality and the old-rent wave.

## 4. Business model and profitability

### Revenue streams
1. **Concierge fees:** recurring, asset-light and cash-positive from month one.
2. **Mediation and success fees:** 2–3% of the transaction value.
3. **Fund spread:** buy shares at a 20–35% discount, exit near full value.
4. **Rental management:** 10–12% of gross rent on activated units.
5. **Finishing and renovation margin:** 10–15% on works.
6. **Partner referrals:** mortgage, insurance and brokerage for consolidated units.
7. **Fund management fees** (later): 2% management + 20% carry on a licensed real-estate fund.

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Market value of share bought | 500,000 |
| Purchase price (25% discount) | 375,000 |

Target returns must beat Egyptian T-bill yields with a healthy margin. Exit-price discipline and fast cycle times are the key KPIs.

## 5. Moat

1. **Proprietary data:** Tarika will build the only dataset on how *fractional, disputed, inherited* property is priced in Egypt. That powers the discount model and the valuation AI.
2. **Legal playbooks:** standardized processes for heirs' declarations, pre-emption notices, POAs from abroad and partition, built with partner law firms.
3. **Capital + trust:** you need both money and a reputation for fairness to be the buyer families accept. That is hard to copy quickly.
4. **Network effects in families:** one estate brings in 5–10 heirs, often across countries. Each one is a future customer for their own parents' estate planning.
5. **Old-rent first-mover:** a 7-year transition window, and whoever builds the operating playbook first owns that market.

## 6. Go-to-market

- **Start narrow:** apartments in Greater Cairo, Giza and Alexandria (clear valuations, liquid end markets). Avoid agricultural land at first, where fragmentation laws and building restrictions add complexity.
- **Diaspora first:** about 14M Egyptians live abroad (Gulf, Europe, US). They are the heirs who feel the pain most, can't follow up in person, and can pay in hard currency. Run targeted campaigns in KSA, UAE, Kuwait and Qatar.
- **Channels:**
  - Facebook and TikTok content in Egyptian Arabic ("How to get your inheritance share without court")
  - A free inheritance calculator (SEO + virality)
  - A referral network of lawyers, notary-adjacent offices and real-estate brokers (paid referral fees)
  - Partnerships with banks' wealth and expat-banking teams
  - Women's rights NGOs for women denied their shares
- **Old-Rent Program:** outreach to heirs of old-rent buildings in Downtown Cairo, Heliopolis, Shubra, Alexandria's Raml Station and similar areas.

## 7. Key risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| **Being seen as exploiting vulnerable heirs** | Published fairness policy: independent third-party valuation, **capped discount**, 14-day cooling-off period, co-heirs always offered first, Sharia advisory board |
| Legal complexity (pre-emption, partition, informal titles) | Always notify co-heirs first; a senior legal partner in the founding team; buy only shares with clean heir declarations; legal opinion on each deal |
| Capital intensity | Start asset-light (concierge + mediation); fund early deals via deal-by-deal SPVs with angel and family-office co-investors; then a fund licensed by the **Financial Regulatory Authority (FRA)** |
| Long holding periods | Strict entry criteria (unit type, location, number of heirs); several exit paths per deal; rental activation to earn yield while holding |
| EGP devaluation and inflation | Real estate is a natural inflation hedge in Egypt; price diaspora services in USD |
| Regulation change | Work proactively with the Ministry of Justice and the Real Estate Registration authority; position Tarika as a tool that **helps register informal property**, which is a stated government goal |
